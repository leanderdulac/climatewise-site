The solution

FIMCE actuarial engine

An ensemble of ML and physical models to price climate risk and detect extreme events.

Multi-source oracle

NOAA, INMET, Embrapa, Open-Meteo and satellite data, with 2-of-3 consensus and verifiable proof.

Parametric payout

A confirmed climate trigger releases the payout: no field inspection, no waiting.

Tokenized policy

ClimatePolicy contract on Base L2 (currently on testnet), traceable end to end.

GPU-accelerated technology

Bidirectional LSTM with multi-head attention for climate and commodity-price forecasting, served on GPUs. With TensorRT optimization we estimate inference tens of times faster and significantly lower cloud cost (estimates, under validation).
